## Land-clearing detection (NSW)

A separate demonstration lives in [`land_clearing/`](land_clearing/): land
clearing detection over New South Wales on Google Earth Engine, with two
pipelines — sequential omnibus change detection on Sentinel-1 for woody
vegetation, and CCDC on Sentinel-1 + Sentinel-2 for forest, woodland and
grassland together. See [`land_clearing/README.md`](land_clearing/README.md).
