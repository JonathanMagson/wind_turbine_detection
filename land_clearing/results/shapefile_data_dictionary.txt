NSW Sentinel-1 land-clearing detections, 2023
=============================================

Generated from ESA Sentinel-1 IW GRDH read from the public AWS mirror, using
the sequential omnibus change detection test of Conradsen et al. (2016).
Geometry EPSG:4326; areas computed in EPSG:3577 (GDA94 / Australian Albers).

Files
-----
nsw_clearing_2023.gpkg            GeoPackage (preferred - full field names)
nsw_clearing_2023.geojson         GeoJSON (web / quick view)
nsw_clearing_2023_shapefile.zip   ESRI Shapefile set (.shp .shx .dbf .prj .cpg)

ATTRIBUTES
----------
clear_id     Polygon identifier, unique across the dataset.
aoi          Area of interest the detection came from.
area_ha      Area in hectares, computed in Australian Albers (equal area).
n_pixels     Number of 89 m detection pixels.
interval     Index of the acquisition interval the change was first detected in.
date_from    Last acquisition BEFORE the change was detected.
date_to      First acquisition AFTER. The change occurred between these two
             dates; the interval is normally 12 days (Sentinel-1 repeat).
vh_pre       Mean VH backscatter (dB) over up to 4 acquisitions before.
vh_post      Mean VH backscatter (dB) over up to 4 acquisitions after.
vh_drop      vh_post - vh_pre. Clearing destroys volume scattering, so this is
             negative; larger magnitude means denser vegetation removed.
vv_pre/vv_post/vv_drop   The same for VV.
persist      Fraction of acquisitions after the break that stay at least 1 dB
             below the pre-break mean. THE MOST USEFUL COLUMN FOR TRIAGE.
             Clearing is permanent, so real events sit at or near 1.0. A
             transient dip from rainfall or a wet-to-dry transition recovers
             and scores low.
rain_flag    'check' if the AOI-wide woody median ALSO fell more than 1 dB in
             the same interval, i.e. the whole area darkened at once. That is a
             real backscatter change but it is not clearing. 'ok' otherwise.
aoi_dlevel   The underlying number behind rain_flag: change in the AOI-wide
             woody median backscatter over the same interval, in dB. Set your
             own threshold if 1 dB does not suit.
n_changes    Number of significant changes detected over the whole series. A
             genuine clearing is normally 1-2; higher suggests an unstable
             pixel rather than a single event.
compact      Polsby-Popper compactness, 4*pi*A/P^2. 1.0 is a circle. Machine
             cleared paddocks are blocky and score moderately; ragged shapes
             are more likely to be speckle survivors.
lc_class     ESA WorldCover 2021 class code (majority within the polygon).
lc_name      Human readable class name.
woody_frac   Fraction of the polygon mapped as woody (tree cover or shrubland)
             in the WorldCover baseline.
lon, lat     Polygon centroid, EPSG:4326.
orbit        Sentinel-1 relative orbit (track). One track per AOI, so incidence
             angle is constant per pixel.
n_scenes     Number of acquisitions in the time series.
multilook    Block-averaging factor applied before testing (8 = 89 m pixels).
enl          Equivalent number of looks used by the test.
alpha        Per-test significance level.

HOW TO READ THESE RESULTS
-------------------------
These are DETECTIONS, not a validated clearing inventory.

* Every polygon has now been checked against cloud-free Sentinel-2 either side
  of its own event window. ONE IS CONFIRMED CLEARING: the 24.9 ha polygon near
  (145.792, -31.543), which tracks surrounding woodland to within 0.3 dB for
  nine months, falls 3.5 dB between 13 September and 7 October 2023 and stays
  down, and shows a discrete vegetated-to-bare transition in the optical.
* THE OTHER FOUR WERE REJECTED on that inspection. All four carry
  rain_flag = 'check', and in each the radar shows backscatter falling across
  the whole chip rather than at the polygon, with no discrete optical change.
  For one of them NDVI actually ROSE while backscatter fell, which is
  vegetation greening as soil dries -- the opposite of clearing.
* Filtering on rain_flag = 'ok' therefore removes every false positive here
  without losing the true one. Do that before using this layer.
* Recall was measured at 83% against that single event, with labels derived
  from the same imagery rather than an independent source. Areas are therefore
  a FLOOR: events below the 2 ha minimum mapping unit are invisible, and
  partially cleared paddocks are clipped.
* No radiometric terrain correction was applied. Results are sound over flat
  inland NSW and unreliable over steep terrain.
* Backscatter loss is not unique to clearing. Fire, harvest and drought dieback
  all reduce it. The woody-cover mask and the flags above suppress most of it,
  but separating fire from clearing needs a burnt-area layer.

For an independent accuracy assessment, compare against the NSW SLATS woody
vegetation change layer from https://datasets.seed.nsw.gov.au/
